# 01 fix 1 (omp/task, high): review findings P1 and P3

## Changed paths
- `src/texttabpfn/scenes.py`: only the interaction branch of `Scene._signal` changed. Each pair term is now `w * 2.54 * tanh(x_i) * tanh(x_j)` instead of `w * x_i * x_j`. The term is bounded, so it has finite variance under Student-t(3). The gain 2.54 = 1/E[tanh(x)^2] for x ~ N(0,1) (measured 2.538): a product of two independent standard-normal parents has unit variance, the same as one unit-weight linear term. The constant `_INTERACTION_GAIN` has a comment.
  - Unchanged: `_make_node`, so the RNG consumption order is the same for every family; calibration; `_forward`; noise; the other mechanisms.
- `src/texttabpfn/report.py`: `capacity_table` checks each row it reads for the requested arm.
  - It raises `IncompleteResults` (exit 1) when `(family, seed)` differs from `config.json["scenes"][scene]`, or when the scene index is outside that list.
  - It also raises when any of `native_logloss`, `optimized_logloss`, `gain`, `val_native` or `val_best` is non-finite.
  - The error message names the CSV line.

## Learnability, evaluation seeds 2000000–2000019 (20 scenes per family)
Script: `/tmp/fix1/learnability.py` (throwaway).
- Model: native raw TabPFN-2.5 through `model.binary_logits`, with the checkpoint's thinking rows, on MPS.
- n = 1024, stratified support (`capacity.stratified_index`) drawn from 4096 support-stream rows; 5 support seeds share 512 query rows.
- Log loss is the mean over scenes. The prior entropy is the mean binary entropy of `base_rate`.
- Low-MI counts cover mb and measure columns with a 20-bin quantile-histogram plug-in MI with y below 0.005 nats, on 20k rows per scene.
- No scene filter or selection rule was added.

| 1 | HEAD = fix | 0.328 | 0.655 | 0.50 | 8/73 | 0/83 | 0.056 / 0.033 |

Family 5 is now about as learnable as family 1:
- Log loss is 0.371 vs 0.328; as a fraction of prior entropy, 0.56 vs 0.50 (0.81 before the fix).
- Low-MI mb columns: 3/84 vs 8/73.

Residual: 8/67 measure columns still have low MI in family 5, as do 12/73 in Gaussian family 4. [INFERENCE] The likely cause is the mechanism class itself, not the noise:
- A single-parent measurement node can draw a pure square term `tanh(h)^2`. That term drops the sign of the hidden cause.
- The same issue appears with Gaussian noise, so heavy tails are not the cause.

These HEAD numbers reproduce the review's evidence closely (seed range 0–9 there, 0–19 here).
