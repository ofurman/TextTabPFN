# Amortized Probabilistic Conditioning for Optimization, Simulation and Inference

[Catalogue](README.md) · [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15320) · [AISTATS proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/chang25a.html) · [PDF, v2](raw/2024_ace.pdf)

## Text-source scope

`no-text` applies: ACE receives structured observations, latent-variable identifiers and values, and numerical prior distributions. Latent marker embeddings identify variables; they do not encode natural-language descriptions or semantic class names. Runtime prior conditioning is not task-domain text input.

## Problem and positioning

Ordinary probabilistic meta-learning learns an implicit training prior. ACE makes selected interpretable latents explicit and allows their values or priors to condition predictions at inference time. Demonstrations include image completion/classification, Bayesian optimization, and simulation-based inference, rather than the proposed binary tabular story benchmark.

## Architecture

MLPs and discrete embeddings encode structured observation pairs and latent tokens. A numerical histogram represents each supplied one-dimensional prior; another MLP embeds it. Stacked transformer layers use context self-attention and target-to-context cross-attention. Gaussian-mixture or categorical heads predict unknown values. Meta-training samples problems, priors, and context/target partitions; prediction uses trained weights without task-specific optimization. Task-relevant latents must be predefined and available during training. The prior interface represents a factorized distribution over latents, not an unrestricted joint prior.

**Block roles:** There is no text-transformer encoder or decoder. `tabular-transformer` denotes the transformer over structured input/output observations and latent tokens; `tabular-row-attention` denotes self-attention across context observations. The interface embeds numerical prior histograms and latent identifiers into that same context, not language embeddings. Target-to-context attention is part of prediction, not a modality cross-attention pooler, and latent tokens are not candidate class-name target tokens.

## Input

Observed covariate/output pairs, query locations, and optional observed latent values or normalized prior histograms over predefined latent ranges. Context and prediction targets may contain both data and latents. Image experiments use coordinates and pixel values rather than a pretrained vision encoder.

## Output

Conditional predictive distributions over continuous or discrete observations and latents. Joint sampling can proceed autoregressively using those conditional distributions.

## For us

ACE is a precedent for adapting amortized prediction to user-supplied information at runtime. A proposed diagnostic is to compare a story-conditioned model with an oracle structured-conditioning model that receives precisely the information expressed by the story. That comparison could separate extraction failures from failures to use a valid prior. An LLM-to-histogram interface would be a new extension, not something ACE implements. Its training assumes generative consistency with supplied priors; robustness to deliberately incorrect stories is therefore not established. Our target protocol must also avoid passing hidden coefficients merely because ACE can accept latent values.
